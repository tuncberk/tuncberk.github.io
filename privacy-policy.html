Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Berk Tunc (“we,” “us,” or “the developer”) creates and operates mobile games and related services (collectively, the “Services”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you use our Services.

This Privacy Policy applies to all games and services published by Berk Tunc, including Hidden Place! and any other apps we publish that link to this policy.

If you have questions, contact us at berktnc@gmail.com.

1) Information We Collect

A) Information collected automatically

Depending on the specific game or service you use, we may automatically collect:

  • Device and App information: device model, OS/version, language, time zone, app version.
  • Identifiers: IP address and device/app identifiers used for analytics, attribution, and security (and where permitted, advertising identifiers such as IDFA on iOS).
  • Usage data (Analytics): gameplay events, session duration, feature usage, and general engagement metrics.
  • Diagnostics (Crash Reporting/Performance): crash logs, diagnostic data, and performance metrics to improve stability and fix bugs.
  • Attribution data: information used to measure install sources and campaign performance (e.g., ad interaction/attribution signals and device/app identifiers).
  • Push notification data: a device-generated push token and your notification preferences (if notifications are used and enabled).

B) Information you provide

If you contact us (e.g., for support), we may collect the information you share such as your email address, message content, and any screenshots/logs you provide.

2) How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate our Services
  • Improve gameplay, features, and user experience
  • Monitor performance, fix crashes, and troubleshoot issues
  • Measure marketing performance and understand install sources (attribution)
  • Send push notifications (if you enable them)
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud/abuse, and comply with legal obligations

3) Sharing of Information

We may share information with trusted service providers that help us run our Services, such as providers for:

  • Analytics
  • Attribution measurement
  • Crash reporting/performance monitoring
  • Push notification delivery

These providers process information on our behalf and only as needed to provide services to us.

We may also share information:

  • To comply with law or legal requests
  • To protect rights and safety of users, the developer, or others
  • During business transfers (e.g., merger, acquisition) as permitted by law

4) Tracking, Advertising Identifiers, and ATT (iOS)

Some of our Services may use device identifiers for attribution and measurement. On iOS, if a game uses data Apple considers “tracking,” iOS may show the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission prompt.

  • If you allow tracking, we (and our measurement partners) may access the IDFA for attribution/measurement.
  • If you deny tracking, we will not access the IDFA, and measurement may be limited.

You can manage this anytime in: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

5) Push Notifications

Some of our games send optional notifications (e.g., reminders, updates). You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings: iOS Settings → Notifications or Android Settings → Apps → Notifications.

6) Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy (e.g., improving services, security, legal compliance). Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data and provider settings.

7) Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8) Children’s Privacy

Our Services are not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact berktnc@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps.

9) International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own depending on where we or our service providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

10) Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable laws (such as GDPR in EU), you may have rights to:

  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To make a request, contact berktnc@gmail.com.

11) Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on tuncberk.github.io and update the Effective Date.
